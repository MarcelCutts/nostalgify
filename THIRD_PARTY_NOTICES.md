## Skins

Nostalgify doesn't include or host any skins. On first launch, the app downloads three starter skins from the
[Winamp Skin Museum](https://skins.webamp.org/), falling back to the
[Internet Archive's Winamp Skin Collection](https://archive.org/details/winampskins). The project website
loads the same three skins from the same places. All rights stay with their authors.

| Skin | Author | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green Dimension V2 | El Argento, 2004 | [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/winampskin_Green-Dimension-V2) |
| Necromech | Peacemaker, for Breed, 2003. Inspired by the art of H.R. Giger | [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/winampskin_Necromech) |
| Kari | Artist unknown. The skin came with no readme or credit | [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/winampskin_Kari) |

If you're one of these authors and want your skin removed from the starter list or the website, please
open an issue and it will be taken down.
